# Assembly-of-Wheel-Loader-
Wheel Loader is a heavy construction machine mainly used for loading, lifting, and transporting materials such as soil, sand, gravel, and debris. This project includes the 3D design, component assembly, and working mechanism of a wheel loader, showing its major parts such as the chassis, wheels, bucket, and hydraulic lifting arms.

## Wheel Loader Assembly – SolidWorks CAD Project:

 ## Project Overview:
This project presents the 3D CAD design and assembly of a Wheel Loader developed using SolidWorks. The model is created by designing individual mechanical components and assembling them to demonstrate the basic structure, mechanism, and working concept of a wheel loader.
The assembly includes major components such as the front body, rear body, bucket, wheels, axle, differential, boom cylinder, boom rod, rocker arm, connecting rods, and other fastening components.

## Project Objective:
To design a complete Wheel Loader assembly using SolidWorks.
To understand the function of individual mechanical components.
To demonstrate the assembly and linkage mechanism.
To understand the basic working of the bucket lifting and dumping mechanism.
To develop practical knowledge of 3D CAD modelling and mechanical assembly.

## Assembly Procedure:
Design all individual components in SolidWorks.
Assemble the wheel, rim and axle to create the wheel sub-assembly.
Assemble the front and rear bodies.
Install the wheel and axle assemblies.
Attach the boom mechanism to the front body.
Install the boom cylinder and boom rod.
Connect the rocker arm and connecting rod.
Attach the bucket to the front linkage.
Add screws and other required connections.
Apply suitable mates and check the movement of the assembly.
Complete and verify the final Wheel Loader assembly.

## Working Principle:
The Wheel Loader operates using two basic systems:
1. Wheel Drive System
Power Source
     ↓
Differential
     ↓
Axle
     ↓
Wheels
     ↓
Machine Movement
2. Loader Mechanism
Hydraulic Cylinder
       ↓
   Boom Rod
       ↓
   Rocker Arm
       ↓
     Bucket
       ↓
Lift → Carry → Dump
The hydraulic cylinder produces the required force to move the loader linkage. The boom and rocker arm transfer this movement to the bucket, allowing it to raise, carry and dump material.
