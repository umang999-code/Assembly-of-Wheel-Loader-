# Final Assembly Components:
The final Wheel Loader assembly is developed by integrating the following components and sub-assemblies:
# Main Components:
Front Body – Front structural frame of the wheel loader.
Rear Body – Rear frame supporting the main machine structure.
Bucket – Used for scooping, lifting, and carrying materials.
Lift Arm – Supports and moves the bucket vertically.
Boom Cylinder – Provides hydraulic movement for lifting the boom and bucket.
Differential – Transfers power to the wheels and enables smooth turning.
# Linkage & Mechanism:
Boom Rod – Provides support and linkage in the lifting mechanism.
Connecting Rod – Connects different moving parts of the mechanism.
Rocker Arm Rod – Transfers motion within the bucket linkage system.
Rocker Arm Screws – Used to securely fasten the rocker arm components.
# Wheel Assembly:
Wheel Sub-Assembly – Complete wheel unit used in the final assembly.
Axle Rod – Supports and connects the wheel/axle mechanism.
Rim – Holds and supports the tire.
Tire – Provides ground contact, traction, and load support.
# Final Integration:
All components are assembled together using appropriate mechanical joints and fasteners to create the complete Wheel Loader Final Assembly. The assembly demonstrates the interaction between the wheel system, differential, lifting mechanism, hydraulic cylinder, linkage system, front body, rear body, and bucket.
